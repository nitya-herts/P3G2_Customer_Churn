## Group rules
1. Attend scheduled meetings and inform the team leader in advance if unable to attend.
2. Respond to group messages within three days, or sooner for urgent matters.
3. Complete assigned tasks by agreed deadlines.
4. Communicate respectfully and consider everyone's ideas fairly.
5. Discuss disagreements openly and use a team vote if necessary.
6. Contribute fairly and support other members when required.
7. Inform the team leader early about difficulties, delays or problems.
8. Submit original, properly referenced work and review it as a team.

## Additional responsibilities and support
Team members may voluntarily tell the team leader about outside responsibilities that affect availability. Any information shared is to be treated respectfully and confidentially. No specific additional responsibilities have been identified at this stage.

Meetings will be planned in advance. Notes and updates will be shared through WhatsApp. Where needed, tasks may be redistributed fairly and reasonable deadline adjustments discussed.

## Unavailability
The team will communicate known periods of unavailability in advance. If a member is temporarily unavailable, essential tasks may be covered fairly by other members. Meeting minutes, WhatsApp updates and shared files will support catch-up on return.

## Review
This agreement should be reviewed and amended by the team if meeting arrangements, availability or responsibilities change.
